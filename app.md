## Full description (verbatim)

Speaker Fix – Water Eject is an iOS app to fix your iPhone speaker after it gets wet. Use safe sound waves to eject trapped water and restore clear, loud audio after rain, showers, spills, or the pool—without visiting a repair shop.

**Who this app is for**
If your iPhone got wet and the speaker sounds quiet, muffled, or distorted, Speaker Fix – Water Eject offers a fast fix at home: targeted sound sessions that push liquid out so you can hear calls, music, and videos clearly again.

**What you can do**
- Run targeted sound sessions to push out trapped liquid and loosen debris
- Test your speaker with low, mid, and high tones
- Check left and right channel balance on speakers and headphones
- Monitor approximate loudness levels in your environment

**Water ejection tool**
Use it when the speaker does not sound normal after getting wet. The app plays tuned sound patterns that move liquid away from the speaker area while you control the volume.

**Speaker test and tones**
After cleaning, play test tones and different frequencies to spot rattling, crackling, or missing ranges and see if you need another cycle.

**When to use the app**
- After splashes, rain, showers, or spills
- When sound suddenly goes quiet, muffled, or distorted
- After using your phone near the pool, kitchen, or bathroom
- When you want to check left and right channels

**Why this method works**
Tuned sound waves push water out of the fine openings around your speaker. You get a safe, repeatable at-home process without harsh shaking or blowing into the device.

**Simple steps**
1. Set volume to a clear, comfortable level.
2. Point the speaker downward.
3. Run a full cleaning session.
4. Repeat if the device was heavily exposed to water.

**Safe use tips**
- Remove cases that trap moisture around the speaker
- Gently move the device while sound plays to help liquid drain
- Wipe the outside before and after the session
- Avoid maximum volume for long periods

**Frequently asked questions**
- Will it damage my device? It uses sound patterns at normal speaker levels; keep volume comfortable.
- Does it fix everything? It helps when liquid or dust affects sound, not deep hardware damage.
- Does it work with headphones? You can test channels; liquid in sealed hardware may still need professional service.

**Compatibility**
- iPhone and iPad with recent iOS versions
- Best on splash-resistant devices
- Also useful for external speakers and audio accessories

**Privacy and pricing**
The app does not modify hardware. Sessions stay on your device. Optional in-app subscription unlocks all features.

**Safety note**
For saltwater, sugary drinks, or serious liquid damage, run multiple sessions, power off when possible, and let the device dry. If sound does not improve, contact a professional repair service.
