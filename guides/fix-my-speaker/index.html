Fix My Speaker: Clear Water and Dust From Your iPhone Speaker

By Vladimir Ivakhnenko, developer of Clear Wave · Updated 2026-09-29

Quick answer

To fix a speaker that sounds muffled or quiet: face the speaker down, set volume to ~75%, and play a low "fix my speaker" tone (≈165 Hz) for 30–60 seconds to push out water or dust. Repeat 2–3 times. Then run a stereo test to confirm both speakers sound the same. If it crackles at every volume, the speaker may be damaged.

"Fix my speaker" usually means one of three things: water got in (sound is muffled), dust and lint are clogging the grille (sound is quiet), or the speaker is damaged (sound crackles or distorts). The first two are fixable at home in minutes. Here's how to tell which one you have and fix it.

How to fix your speaker in 5 steps

  1. Identify the symptom. Muffled or "underwater" = water. Quiet but clean = dust or lint. Crackling or buzzing at all volumes = possible damage.
  2. Remove the case and wipe the grille. Use a dry, lint-free cloth. For dust, a soft dry toothbrush brushed gently across the grille helps.
  3. Play a low-frequency fix tone. Face the speaker down, volume 70–80%, play the tone for 30–60 seconds.
  4. Repeat and wipe. Run 2–3 cycles, wiping any droplets or dust that appear.
  5. Test both channels. Play sound through the left and right speakers separately and compare.

Quick software checks before you clean

  • Make sure the phone isn't connected to AirPods or a Bluetooth speaker (Control Center → AirPlay icon).
  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons while playing media.
  • Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics and make sure the ringer volume isn't at zero.
  • Restart the iPhone — it fixes audio routing glitches surprisingly often.

Questions

Does "fix my speaker" work for Android too?

The technique works on any phone speaker. Clear Wave itself is for iPhone and iPad; on Android, use a similar tone-based app.

How many times should I run a fix my speaker tone?

Usually 2–3 cycles. After heavy exposure (pool, toilet), up to 5 cycles with a few minutes of drying between them.

Can I fix my speaker if it is blown?

No app can repair a torn membrane or burnt voice coil. Clear Wave can help you diagnose it so you know whether a repair is needed.
