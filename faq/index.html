Water eject & speaker FAQ

Short, honest answers — each with a full explanation one click away.

What is Clear Wave?

Clear Wave is a free iPhone and iPad app (App Store name: “Speaker Fix – Water Eject”) that pushes water and dust out of your speaker with tuned sound waves, then checks the result with a stereo test, tone generator and dB meter.

Learn more about What is Clear Wave →

Does water eject actually work?

Yes — for water trapped in the speaker grille, which causes most muffled sound after rain, showers or splashes. Low-frequency sound makes the speaker membrane push droplets out; you can often see them appear on the mesh.

Learn more about Does water eject actually work →

Is water eject safe for my iPhone speaker?

Yes. A water eject tone is ordinary audio played at normal volume, like music. Keep the volume around 70–80%, avoid long runs at maximum, and stop if you hear harsh rattling.

Learn more about Is water eject safe for my iPhone speaker →

How long does it take for water to leave an iPhone speaker?

Left alone, several hours to a full day. With a water eject tone, most of it comes out in 1–3 cycles of 30–60 seconds; heavy exposure may need up to 5 cycles plus air-drying.

Learn more about How long does it take for water to leave an iPhone speaker →

Should I put my wet iPhone in rice?

No. Apple specifically advises against it because small rice particles can get into the iPhone. Tap it gently with the connector facing down, let it air-dry, and use a water eject tone for the speaker.

Learn more about Should I put my wet iPhone in rice →

What frequency gets water out of a speaker?

Low frequencies around 150–200 Hz work best; 165 Hz is the most widely used. Low tones make the speaker membrane move further on each stroke, pushing water through the grille.

Learn more about What frequency gets water out of a speaker →

Can water eject fix a blown speaker?

No — a truly blown speaker is physically damaged and needs replacement. But many speakers that sound "blown" are just wet or clogged, and water eject fixes those. Test before you pay for a repair.

Learn more about Can water eject fix a blown speaker →

Does water eject work on AirPods?

Only partly. You can play a water eject tone through connected AirPods, but their drivers are small and sealed, so the effect is limited. Dry them with a lint-free cloth, let them air-dry, then test left and right channels.

Learn more about Does water eject work on AirPods →

Does the iPhone have a built-in water eject?

No. Only Apple Watch has a built-in water eject (Water Lock). On iPhone you need a water eject app like Clear Wave or a community Siri shortcut.

Learn more about Does the iPhone have a built-in water eject →

How many times should I run water eject?

2–3 cycles of 30–60 seconds for splashes, rain or shower steam. Up to 5 cycles after submersion (pool, toilet), with a few minutes of drying between them. Stop when a stereo test sounds clear on both sides.

Learn more about How many times should I run water eject →

Is Clear Wave free?

Clear Wave is free to download on iPhone and iPad. An optional in-app subscription (with a free trial) unlocks full access to all tools: water eject, stereo test, tone generator and dB meter.
